How it works

From video to dinner in four steps.

You found the adobo that tastes like home. Kawali gets it out of the video and into your kitchen.

  1. 1

    Paste

    Copy the link to a Filipino cooking video and paste it into Kawali. Or skip the copying and share it to Kawali straight from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest or Safari.

  2. 2

    Check

    Kawali reads the recipe from the description, the linked recipe page and the narration, in English, Tagalog or Taglish. Look it over and fix anything before you save.

  3. 3

    Shop

    Add the ingredients to Groceries. Kawali sorts them by aisle and adds up the same ingredient across recipes, so nothing is forgotten at the store.

  4. 4

    Cook

    Follow the steps at the stove, like cooking beside Mom, with the creator credited and the original video one tap away.

Built for Filipino food

Not a recipe app with a Filipino filter.

Kawali was made for Filipino cooking from the first line: the words, the ingredients, the way a tita tells you how much patis to add.

Knows its patis from its bagoong.

Kawali reads Filipino cooking the way it's really talked about, in English, Tagalog or Taglish, with a “konting patis lang” here and an “igisa ang bawang” there.

Ingredient names stay the way your family says them. Patis stays patis, with “fish sauce” beside it only as an explanation, and bagoong alamang never turns into bagoong isda.

Nothing invented.

When the cook says “konting patis lang”, Kawali writes down patis and leaves the amount blank. It never guesses a quantity, a cooking time or how many it serves, and it tells you what the video left out.

Quantity for patis was not specified. The narration may have misheard one ingredient.

Even when nobody talks.

No narration and no recipe in the caption? Kawali watches the video itself to work out the steps. Save the recipe and get on with your day; Kawali lets you know when the steps are in.

Groceries

Nothing forgotten at the grocery store.

Add a recipe's ingredients in one tap, keep only the ones you need, and shop the whole week from one list.

  • Choose what to add

    Optional ingredients start unchecked, and anything already on your list from that recipe is skipped.

  • Sorted by aisle

    Produce, Meat, Seafood, Filipino / Asian, Rice / Noodles, Pantry, Spices and more, so you walk the store once.

  • Added up for you

    Four cloves of bawang for the adobo and six for the sinigang become one line, ten cloves, with each recipe it's for listed underneath. Amounts in different units stay on their own lines.

  • English names when you need them

    Shopping somewhere that says “eggplant”, not “talong”? Show English names with one tap.

  • Check off, clear, print

    Tick items off as you shop, clear what's done, or print the list for the fridge door.

Privacy

No ads. No tracking.

Your recipes and your grocery list live on your iPhone. Kawali only sends out what it needs to read a recipe, and never anything that says who you are.

Your kitchen stays on your phone

Saved recipes, your edits and notes, the pictures and the grocery list are stored on your iPhone.

Only the recipe travels

To read a video, Kawali sends its link to our server, which reads the public post. Photos are read on your iPhone, and only the words in them are sent.

Nothing to track

No ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking in the app. We don't sell or share data, and we don't know which recipes are yours.
